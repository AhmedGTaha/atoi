Why
ATOI

You talk to the people building it

No account managers relaying messages. The designer and the engineer are in the room, and they answer the same day.

We build around the actual problem

Sometimes the answer is a smaller product than you asked for. We'll say that before you pay for the bigger one.

Design and engineering, not one then the other

Interfaces are designed with the data model in mind, so what you approve is what can actually be built.

Code someone else can pick up

Documented, tested, and yours. If you hire an in-house team next year, they inherit something readable.

No unnecessary complexity

Fewer moving parts, fewer things to break, fewer invoices for maintaining something that shouldn't exist.

Our position

Software is not a deliverable. It is something a person opens on a Tuesday to get their job done — and it should solve a real problem, feel good in the hand, and still be maintainable two years after we've left.

ATOI is a small studio in Manama working with businesses and founders across the Gulf and beyond. Small on purpose: the people who scope your project are the people who build it.

We take on a handful of projects at a time. That is a constraint, and it is also the reason the work is good.
